WORKING TOGETHER 03 — TERMS OF USE

Fair expectations.
Clear responsibilities.

This overview is for general information. The signed proposal or project agreement for your specific project takes priority where applicable.

01

Scope & changes

Only deliverables written in the accepted proposal are included. Extra features, revisions or changes may affect price and timeline.

02

Fees & third-party costs

Payment milestones and refund/cancellation terms should be stated in writing. Domains, hosting, paid APIs and third-party licences may cost extra.

03

Ownership & licences

Ownership and handover rights for final work depend on the project agreement and full payment. Open-source and third-party components keep their own licence terms.

04

Client responsibilities

Clients must provide lawful content, permissions, accurate information and timely feedback, and protect account credentials.

05

Privacy & security

Data handling, access, retention and privacy duties should be agreed for the project. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

06

Support & availability

Maintenance, backups, updates and response times are included only when agreed. Third-party outages or policy changes may affect service.

07

Warranties & liability

Any warranties and liability limits must be set out in the project agreement and remain subject to applicable law.

08

Law & disputes

The applicable governing law and dispute process should be specified in the signed project agreement. Mandatory legal rights remain unaffected.
